Match Strategy
T20 Cricket · Flagship
Match Simulator & Opponent Strategy Prediction
Phase-wise tactical prediction system that models team behavior using historical ball-by-ball data. Anticipates powerplay aggression, bowling plans, and death-over strategies before patterns fully emerge in live matches.
Flagship System
Modeling Approach
  • Context-aware feature engineering (phase, venue, opposition, player roles)
  • Random Forest for phase-wise pattern learning
  • XGBoost for optimized tactical classification
  • Team-specific matchup modeling (e.g., CSK vs RCB)
Insights Enabled
  • Predictive understanding of phase-wise intent
  • Scenario-based tactical simulation
  • Pre-match and in-match planning support
  • Stress-test match plans before game day

End-to-End · ODI · Flagship
ODI Full Match Predictor
Three-stage live win-probability engine: pre-match (calibrated LR) → 1st innings score projection (GBM + quantile bands) → 2nd innings chase (calibrated XGBoost · 91% accuracy in death overs). Trained on 2,520 ODIs & 1.3M deliveries from Cricsheet. ICC-style Elo with variable K + recency weighting. Calibrated probabilities (Brier 0.146) for honest decision support.

Player Impact & Evaluation
T20 Cricket · Flagship
Impact Index & Auction Strategy Model
Traditional stats fail to capture a player's true influence. This composite impact model combines batting contribution, bowling efficiency, fielding value, and contextual weighting (chases, death overs, pressure) into a single decision metric.
Composite Model
Modeling Components
  • Batting contribution & partnership impact
  • Bowling efficiency & pressure creation index
  • Fielding value estimation
  • Contextual weighting: chases, death overs, pressure phases
Applications
  • Auction & squad planning intelligence
  • Role-based player deployment decisions
  • Fantasy team optimization
  • Performance benchmarking across formats
